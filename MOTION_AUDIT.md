# Motion Audit (`MOTION_AUDIT.md`)

**Project:** AI Document Analyser — AICTE Scrutiny & Regulatory Compliance Portal (SIH26261)  
**Branch:** `feat/ui-motion-auth`  
**Skills Applied:** `find-animation-opportunities`, `animation-vocabulary`, `emil-design-eng`, `design-taste-frontend`

---

## Operating Premise

This audit evaluates every screen and interactive surface against the 4-part Motion Gate from `find-animation-opportunities` and `emil-design-eng`:
1. **Frequency:** High-frequency (100+/day) and keyboard-driven actions never animate; occasional state transitions earn concise, purposeful motion.
2. **Purpose:** Every surviving animation must serve **Feedback**, **State indication**, **Spatial consistency**, or **Preventing a jarring change** — never decoration.
3. **Speed:** All UI transitions stay strictly under `300ms` (`100–160ms` press feedback, `150–200ms` popovers/collapses, `180–260ms` state resolution).
4. **Function:** Information-dense regulatory data (application IDs, FSR ratios, handbook clauses) must remain rock-solid while reading.

## Part 3 — Audit Verdict

The application's core functional flows are well-structured, and its restraint on high-frequency navigation is already sound. The highest-leverage improvements are:
1. Fixing the broken/missing modal and popover entrance/exit transitions (`PdfSnippetModal`, `FieldValidationCard` `(i)` popover).
2. Replacing the paint-heavy `1.1s` `boxShadow` keyframe and `scale(0.4)` bounce on `FieldValidationCard` with a crisp, composite-friendly damped spring (`stiffness: 380, damping: 28`) so **Scenario D's Re-verify and Auto-Fix transitions** feel instantaneous, physical, and trustworthy.
3. Smoothing height/layout jumps on the collapsible **API Log** panel and streaming **Agent Activity** log.
